Dental hygiene for children, at home

A cleaning at home on your child's terms — no waiting room, no rush, and one-on-one time to make the visit genuinely engaging. Serving families across Windsor and Essex County.

Why an early visit matters

  • Catch problems early. Early signs of tooth decay or improper jaw development can be spotted long before they become painful or costly.
  • Build comfort. Early, gentle visits help your child feel safe and relaxed about dental care instead of anxious about it.
  • Guidance for parents. Learn the best ways to clean tiny teeth and how to manage habits like thumb-sucking.

What every visit includes

  • Gentle first visits
  • Hands-on oral health instruction
  • Models to play and learn with
  • Brushing they can practise
  • Gentle scaling and tartar removal
  • Fluoride to protect new teeth

Coverage for children

Healthy Smiles Ontario covers preventive dental care for eligible children and youth aged 17 and under, including cleanings, fluoride, and sealants, billed directly to the program. See the Insurance page.

Children's hygiene care, without the trip

For busy parents, a cleaning shouldn't mean an afternoon off work, a car seat battle, and a waiting room full of strangers. Care happens in your living room, on a schedule that fits around school and naps.

Because it's one-on-one with no one waiting, there's room to make it genuinely engaging. Children get plenty of hands-on oral hygiene instruction, brushing and flossing practice they can actually keep up, and time to ask questions until they feel comfortable.

By age one The First Visit

Children should have their first dental visit within six months of their first tooth appearing, or by their first birthday, whichever comes first.
