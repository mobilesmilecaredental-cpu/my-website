Children’s dental hygiene, at home

A cleaning on your child’s terms: no waiting room, no rush, and one-on-one time to make it fun.

Book a visit Call (905) 941-7580

Why home works for kids

A cleaning shouldn’t mean an afternoon off work, a car-seat battle and a waiting room full of strangers.

Care happens in your living room, on a schedule that fits around school and naps. Because it’s one-on-one with no one waiting, there’s time to make it engaging: plenty of hands-on brushing and flossing practice, and room for questions until your child feels comfortable.

  • Visits at home, around school and nap times
  • A familiar, playful setting instead of a clinic chair
  • One-on-one attention, with nothing rushed
  • Brushing and flossing they can actually keep up
By age one

When to book the first visit

Children should have their first dental visit within six months of their first tooth appearing, or by their first birthday, whichever comes first. Early, gentle visits catch problems before they hurt, help your child feel safe about dental care, and give you practical tips for cleaning tiny teeth and managing habits like thumb-sucking.

What a children’s visit includes

Every visit is adapted to your child’s age and comfort.

Gentle cleaning
Careful scaling and tartar removal, at your child’s pace.
Fluoride varnish
Helps protect new teeth from decay.
Sealants
A protective coating for the grooves of back teeth.
Hands-on instruction
Brushing and flossing practice, with models to play and learn with.
Guidance for parents
What to watch for, and how to make home care easier.
Referrals
A trusted dentist when anything needs more care.

Coverage for kids

Many families qualify for fully funded children’s dental care. I bill these programs directly:

  • Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) for eligible children in qualifying households
  • Healthy Smiles Ontario for children and youth 17 and under in eligible households
  • Private insurance, with direct billing to most major providers
