On-site dental hygiene for long-term care & retirement homes

Smile Care delivers dental hygiene care inside long-term care homes, retirement residences, assisted living, and memory care units across Windsor and Essex County. Residents are seen in their own rooms or a shared common space, with no transport to arrange and minimal demand on nursing staff.

How it works for your facility

  • All equipment is brought on-site, including portable chairs and sterilized instruments.
  • Visits are scheduled around meals, medication rounds, and activity programming.
  • Consent, medical history, and charting are handled directly with families and staff.
  • Direct billing to insurance and government plans, including NIHB, CDCP, and ODSP.
  • Recurring visit schedules available for ongoing resident care.

Services provided on-site

  • Personalized oral hygiene review
  • Removal of soft and hard deposits
  • Removal of extrinsic stains
  • Fluoride varnish treatments
  • Pit and fissure sealants
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Interim stabilization therapy
  • Silver diamine fluoride
  • Referrals to health care providers

Service area

Windsor · LaSalle · Tecumseh · Amherstburg · Essex · Kingsville · Leamington · Lakeshore · Belle River · Harrow · McGregor · Comber
