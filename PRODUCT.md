## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` with Cloudflare in front (www.mobilesmilecare.ca). Redesign work lives on the `redesign` branch until launch.

## Users

- Adults who would rather not travel to a clinic: busy professionals (home or workplace visits) and people who value the comfort of home.
- Families booking for others: parents booking children; adult children booking for a senior parent or a relative with dementia or limited mobility.
- Long-term care and retirement home staff arranging on-site hygiene for residents.
- Many visitors are older or read on a phone, so legibility and an obvious phone number matter.

## Product Purpose

Mobile Smile Care: Dental Hygiene is a one-person mobile dental hygiene practice. Lisa Suciu, RDH, brings a fully equipped portable hygiene setup to homes, workplaces and care residences across Windsor and Essex County. The site succeeds when a visitor trusts her quickly and books, calls or asks a question, and when it ranks for local mobile hygiene searches.

## Positioning

A registered hygienist comes to you with clinic-grade portable equipment. One clinician from first visit to last, at your pace, in your own space. It's a boutique service: cozy but premium. Direct billing, including government programs.

## Operating Context

- Booking, intake and medical-history forms run through Akitu One. The booking link isn't known yet, so Book buttons use a swappable placeholder.
- The website's contact form (Web3Forms → Gmail) is for general questions and care-home inquiries only. No health information goes through it.
- Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics, injected automatically by Cloudflare. No snippet in code.
- Hours: Fri 6–9 pm (inferred pm), Sat 10 am–8 pm, Sun 10 am–6 pm.
- Service area: Windsor and Essex County only (Windsor, LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Lakeshore/Belle River, Essex, Kingsville, Leamington, Harrow, McGregor, Comber and nearby). A small travel fee is discussed after booking; it isn't published.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: oral hygiene review, scaling (soft and hard deposit removal), stain removal/polishing, fluoride varnish, pit and fissure sealants, oral cancer screening, interim stabilization therapy, silver diamine fluoride, referrals.
- Billing: direct billing to most private plans; CDCP, NIHB and ODSP accepted.
- She is a dental hygienist, not a dentist. Never call her a dentist or imply dentist services.
- No prices, no CDHO number and no testimonials on the site (Ontario regulator advertising caution).
- Keep the existing page URLs (indexed): index, children, busy-professionals, seniors-mobility, care-homes, insurance, patient-forms. A Meet Lisa page is new.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first: her face, her credentials and plain facts before persuasion.
2. Every page offers the next step (book, call, ask) without hunting.
3. Say less: one clear sentence beats three soft ones.
4. Local search is the growth engine: accurate, consistent facts everywhere.
5. Fast on a phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Older visitors and low-vision readers are core: body text at least 18px, high contrast, large tap targets, no meaning carried by motion alone, and reduced-motion respected.
