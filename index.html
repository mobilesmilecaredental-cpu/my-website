Dental hygiene services in Windsor & Essex

Preventive and therapeutic dental hygiene, delivered with the same standard of care as a clinic.

Oral hygiene review
A look at your gums and teeth, with practical advice on brushing, flossing and products.
Scaling
Thorough removal of plaque and tartar to protect your gums.
Stain removal & polishing
Lifts surface stains for a cleaner, brighter finish.
Oral cancer screening
A careful check of the soft tissues, because early detection matters.
Fluoride varnish
Strengthens enamel and eases sensitivity.
Pit & fissure sealants
A protective coating for the grooves of back teeth.
Silver diamine fluoride
A non-invasive treatment that arrests decay, useful when drilling isn’t an option.
Interim stabilization therapy
Temporary care to stabilize active decay until further treatment.
Referrals
When you need more, I connect you with trusted dentists and specialists.

Questions, answered

What is mobile dental hygiene?
Professional dental hygiene care brought to you. Instead of travelling to a clinic, I come to your home, workplace or care residence with all the equipment needed for a complete, comfortable visit.
Are you a dentist?
No. I’m a Registered Dental Hygienist. I provide cleanings and preventive care, and refer you to a trusted dentist for treatment such as fillings or extractions.
What do I need for a home visit?
Very little: a comfortable place to sit or recline, an electrical outlet and a nearby sink. I bring all the equipment and follow strict infection-control protocols.
How does a mobile visit compare to a clinic visit?
You get a complete hygiene appointment (assessment, scaling and polishing) with clinic-grade portable equipment, sterilized instruments, and the same professional and infection-control standards that apply in any clinic.
Do you bill my insurance directly?
Yes. Direct billing is available for most private plans, and for the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), NIHB, ODSP and Healthy Smiles Ontario. See insurance details.
Am I eligible for the CDCP?
The Canadian Dental Care Plan covers eligible Canadian residents who meet income requirements and don’t have access to private dental insurance. I’m happy to help you check your coverage.
Do you visit long-term care and retirement homes?
Yes. I provide on-site care in long-term care homes, retirement residences, assisted living and memory care, with no resident transport. Details for care homes.
What areas do you serve?
Windsor and Essex County, including LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Lakeshore, Belle River, Essex, Kingsville, Leamington, Harrow, McGregor and Comber. Not sure if I reach you? Just ask.
Can my visit be in Romanian?
Yes. I speak English and Romanian, so your visit can be in whichever you prefer.
Is there a travel fee?
A small travel fee may apply depending on where you are. We’ll confirm it together when you book.
