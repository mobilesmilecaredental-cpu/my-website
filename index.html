Mobile dental hygiene in Windsor & Essex County

Smile Care brings comprehensive dental hygiene and preventive care to your home, workplace, or care residence across Windsor and Essex County. Care is provided by a registered dental hygienist, with direct billing to most insurance and government plans.

Services

  • Personalized oral hygiene review. A tailored assessment of your oral health with practical guidance on brushing, flossing, and the products that work best for you.
  • Removal of soft and hard deposits. Gentle, thorough scaling to remove plaque and tartar, protecting your gums from disease.
  • Removal of extrinsic stains. Polishing that lifts surface stains for a cleaner, brighter feeling.
  • Fluoride varnish treatments. Strengthens enamel and eases sensitivity to help protect teeth against decay.
  • Pit and fissure sealants. A protective coating for the grooves of the teeth, guarding vulnerable surfaces from cavities.
  • Oral cancer screening. A careful examination of the soft tissues, because early detection makes all the difference.
  • Interim stabilization therapy. Temporary care to stabilize and manage active decay when comprehensive treatment is not yet possible.
  • Silver diamine fluoride. A non-invasive treatment that arrests decay and relieves sensitivity.
  • Referrals to health care providers. Connections to trusted dentists and specialists for seamless continued care.

Who we help

  • Children — gentle first visits at home with no waiting room.
  • Busy professionals — appointments at home or at your workplace.
  • Seniors & mobility — unhurried, patient-centered visits.
  • Long-term care homes — on-site hygiene for residents.

Insurance and direct billing

All insurance and government plans are accepted, including Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB), the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), and the Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP). Full details on the Insurance page.

Frequently asked questions

What is mobile dental hygiene?

Professional dental hygiene care brought directly to you. Instead of travelling to a clinic, care comes to your home, residence, or care facility with all the equipment needed for a complete, comfortable visit.

Who is mobile care best for?

Anyone who finds it difficult to reach a clinic: seniors, people with limited mobility, those with demanding schedules, or anyone who simply feels more at ease receiving care at home.

What do I need for a home visit?

Very little. A comfortable space to sit or recline, access to an electrical outlet, and a nearby sink. All necessary equipment is brought along, following strict infection-control protocols.

Do you offer direct billing?

Yes. Direct billing is offered, and all insurance and government plans are accepted, including NIHB, the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), and ODSP.

Am I eligible for the CDCP?

The Canadian Dental Care Plan is available to eligible Canadian residents who meet income requirements. Get in touch for help understanding your coverage.

Do you provide care in long-term care and retirement homes?

Yes. Hygiene care is delivered on-site in long-term care homes, retirement residences, assisted living, and memory care units, with no resident transport and minimal demand on staff.

What areas do you serve?

Mobile dental hygiene throughout Windsor and Essex County. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific area.
