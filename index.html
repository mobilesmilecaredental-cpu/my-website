Welcome to Smile Care

Comprehensive hygiene, wherever you are

Providing comprehensive dental hygiene and preventive care designed to keep your smile healthy and confident. From routine cleanings to advanced treatments, I make oral care simple, convenient, and stress-free for everyone.

As a Registered Dental Hygienist, I take the time to understand your needs and put you at ease, bringing the same standard of clinical care you'd expect from a clinic, in the comfort of your own space.

What I Offer

Services

A full range of preventive and therapeutic dental hygiene services, each delivered with care and attention to your comfort.

Personalized Oral Hygiene Review

A tailored assessment of your oral health with practical guidance on brushing, flossing, and the products that work best for you.

Removal of Soft & Hard Deposits

Gentle, thorough scaling to remove plaque and tartar, protecting your gums from disease.

Removal of Extrinsic Stains

Polishing that lifts surface stains for a cleaner, brighter, fresh-from-the-clinic feeling.

Fluoride Varnish Treatments

Strengthens enamel and eases sensitivity to help protect your teeth against decay.

Pit & Fissure Sealants

A protective coating for the grooves of the teeth, guarding vulnerable surfaces from cavities.

Oral Cancer Screening

A careful examination of the soft tissues, because early detection makes all the difference.

Interim Stabilization Therapy

Temporary care to stabilize and manage active decay when comprehensive treatment isn’t yet possible.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

A non-invasive treatment that arrests decay and relieves sensitivity, ideal for gentle care.

Referrals to Health Care Providers

When you need more, I’ll connect you with trusted dentists and specialists for seamless continued care.
