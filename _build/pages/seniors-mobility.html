Care that comes to them

For many of my patients, getting to a clinic is difficult or stressful. So I bring the appointment home.

Caring for seniors, people living with dementia, and anyone with reduced mobility is a big part of why I went mobile. Every visit allows time for frequent breaks and a soothing pace, so each person feels respected and comfortable from start to finish.

  • Unhurried appointments with time for breaks
  • Experience caring for people living with dementia
  • Care in a familiar chair, in a familiar room
  • Comfort, dignity and trust at every step

Booking for a parent?

Many visits are arranged by a son, daughter or caregiver. You can book on their behalf, share anything that helps me prepare, and be there for the visit if that makes them more comfortable.

Afterward I’ll explain what was done and share home-care tips, with a referral to a trusted dentist if further treatment is needed.
