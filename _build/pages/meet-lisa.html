I’m a Registered Dental Hygienist, and I bring dental hygiene care to people across Windsor & Essex County, in their homes, workplaces and care residences.

Vorbesc română.Care in English or Romanian.

Why I went mobile

I went mobile to serve the people who need care most: those who can’t easily get to a clinic on their own, and those who would simply prefer the comfort of their own home.

Seeing patients where they live changes the appointment. There’s no waiting room and no rush, and I get to know the person in the chair. It’s one hygienist from your first visit to your last.
