## Scope
Home page (index.html) of the full-site redesign; its world carries to every page. Mode: Persuade.

## Surface strategy
- Audience: people booking for themselves or for a child, parent or care-home resident in Windsor–Essex. Many are older or on a phone.
- Job: trust Lisa within seconds, find "my situation", then book, call or ask.
- Proof: real photos of Lisa, her portable setup and a consenting patient; her credentials (RDH, Niagara College 2019, 7 years, Romanian and English); direct billing (CDCP, NIHB, ODSP, private plans).
- Constraints: user-pinned A+C hybrid (personal photo-led hero, plus a "Who's the visit for?" router into the audience pages). User-pinned warm-neutral and bronze tones matching the logo. Home page about 700 words. Keep URLs and SEO. Unattended run: the user said proceed without approvals, so the roll's assigned candidate was taken inside the pinned palette.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The practice's calling card, left at your door. Refuses the generic "spa-cream hero + three feature cards + testimonial carousel" dental template.
OWN-WORLD: Fine stationery from a boutique house-call practice. Porcelain card stock (the logo's own #F2F2F1 ground), espresso ink (her black scrubs) owning whole bands, bronze foil (the logo line) as hairlines, rules and the halo arc. Engraved-letterhead small caps for labels, a sharp classical serif for display, a highly legible sans for reading. Edges are crisp; corners are small (2–4px) like trimmed card; no glass, no gradients except the logo's single warm halo.
STORY: She is a real, credentialed person (face first), she comes to you (the setup is real), here's your path (router), it's covered (billing), and booking is one tap.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns on desktop. Left: small-caps kicker "Mobile dental hygienist · Windsor & Essex", a large serif H1, one sentence, primary "Book a visit" (espresso), secondary phone link, then a short bronze-ruled credentials line. Right: Lisa's portrait (IMG_0358) in a tall trimmed frame under a thin bronze halo arc echoing the logo. On mobile, the photo goes first and is cropped tighter, with the H1 and both actions visible without scrolling past one screen.
FORM: Calling-card stationery, candidate 3 of 7 (porcelain & bronze letterhead, house-call bag, calling card, concierge menu, instrument cassette, living-room clinic, apothecary label), seed 08b89639. Raises: from the darkroom bay, honest fixed stations for "How a visit works" (book, arrive, care, plan, in order). From the OSPAAAL poster, a bilingual greeting (English/Romanian) set as a typographic line, because she really speaks both. From the jacquard brocade, the espresso band carries bronze foil hairlines with total commitment. Signature interaction: the router rows behave like cards drawn from a holder; hover or focus slides the row and sweeps a bronze foil rule across it, with reduced-motion fallback.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
