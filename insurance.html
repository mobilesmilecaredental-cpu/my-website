Insurance & direct billing for mobile dental hygiene

Mobile dental hygiene is covered by most dental plans in the same way a clinic visit is. Smile Care offers direct billing so there is usually nothing to pay up front and no forms to submit yourself.

Plans accepted

  • Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB) — direct billing available.
  • Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) — accepted for eligible residents.
  • Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) — direct billing available.
  • Most major insurance providers — direct billing with select plans.
  • Healthy Smiles Ontario — coverage for eligible children and youth aged 17 and under.

No coverage?

Care is available on a straightforward fee-for-service basis, with costs explained before any treatment begins.

The Short Version

What direct billing actually means

Instead of paying the full amount and waiting weeks for reimbursement, the claim goes to your insurer first. You only pay whatever portion your plan doesn't cover. Since the exact services you need aren't known until your teeth are actually assessed, you'll get a ballpark range beforehand rather than a fixed number.
