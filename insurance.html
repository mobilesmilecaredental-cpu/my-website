Insurance & direct billing

Dental benefits can be confusing. Here’s how coverage works, what direct billing means for you, and how to find out what you’re entitled to.

Ask about my coverage Call (905) 941-7580

What direct billing means

Instead of paying the full amount and waiting for a reimbursement cheque, the claim goes to your insurer first. You pay only what your plan doesn’t cover.

  1. Your coverage is checked first

    Before the appointment, your plan details are confirmed so you know roughly what’s covered.

  2. The claim is submitted for you

    After the visit, it goes straight to your insurer or program. No forms to chase.

  3. You pay only the remainder

    The exact services aren’t known until your teeth are assessed, so you’ll get a ballpark range beforehand and the final figure after the visit.

Government programs

Many people qualify for fully funded care without realizing it. All of these are billed directly.

Federal

Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)

Covers eligible Canadian residents who meet the income requirements and don’t have access to private dental insurance.

First Nations & Inuit

Non-Insured Health Benefits (NIHB)

Covers registered First Nations and recognized Inuit clients, including hygiene visits.

Provincial

Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)

ODSP recipients and eligible family members are covered for dental hygiene care.

For kids

Healthy Smiles Ontario (HSO)

Preventive care for children and youth 17 and under in eligible households, including cleanings, fluoride and sealants.

In some cases coverage can be combined, such as a private plan alongside a government program. Bring both and I’ll coordinate them for you.

Three things to check with your insurer

A two-minute call answers all of these and means no surprises later. Not sure how to ask? I’ll talk you through it.

How often are you covered?
Most plans cover a hygiene visit every six or nine months.
What’s your annual maximum?
Plans usually cap what they pay each year, and unused benefits typically expire.
What percentage is covered?
Preventive care is often covered at 80 to 100 percent.
