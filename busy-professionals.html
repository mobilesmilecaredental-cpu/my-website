How it works

If a cleaning has been on your list for a year or two, you're in good company. Appointments come to your home or workplace, so there's no commute, no time off, and no waiting room.

The visit itself is no different from the hygiene appointment you would have at a clinic: a full assessment, scaling and polishing, and honest guidance on what needs attention, with a referral to a trusted dentist if anything more is needed.
