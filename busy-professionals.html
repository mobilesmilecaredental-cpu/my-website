Dental cleanings at home or at work

A full hygiene appointment that comes to you. No commute, no time off, no waiting room.

Book a visit Call (905) 941-7580

The cleaning you keep putting off, handled

If a cleaning has been on your list for a year or two, you’re in good company.

I come to your home or your workplace, so the appointment fits into your day instead of taking it over. The visit itself is the same one you’d have at a clinic: a full assessment, scaling and polishing, and straightforward advice on anything that needs attention, with a referral to a trusted dentist if you need more.

  • Appointments at home or at your workplace
  • Friday evenings and weekend hours
  • Assessment, scaling and polishing in one visit
  • Direct billing to most insurance and government plans

How it works

  1. Book a time that suits you

    Online or by phone. Your intake forms arrive by email before the visit.

  2. I set up where you are

    All you need is a comfortable seat, an outlet and a sink nearby.

  3. Your cleaning, start to finish

    A complete appointment without the travel, then you’re back to your day.

  4. Insurance billed for you

    The claim goes straight to your plan, so there’s no paperwork to chase.

Not sure what your plan covers?

Most workplace plans cover a hygiene visit every six or nine months. I’ll help you check before you book.
